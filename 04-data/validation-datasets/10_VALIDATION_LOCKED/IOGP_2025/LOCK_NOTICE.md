# Validation lock: IOGP 2025

IOGP 2025 high-potential and fatal-incident narrative reports are reserved as the initial external validation candidate once the authorized manual download is completed. Until then, this folder contains the lock policy only and no inspected report records.

Locked records must not be used for fitting, threshold tuning, prompt examples, synthetic augmentation, feature engineering based on answer inspection, or hyperparameter optimization.
