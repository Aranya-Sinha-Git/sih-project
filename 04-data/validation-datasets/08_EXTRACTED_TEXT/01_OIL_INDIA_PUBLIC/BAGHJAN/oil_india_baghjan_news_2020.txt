Oil India Ltd won four blocks out of the 11 oil and gas 
exploration blocks offered for bidding in the latest 
licensing OALP Bid Round-V offered by the Government. 
OIL won two blocks each in Rajasthan and the Assam 
shelf & Assam basins in the round. Oil and Natural 
Gas Corp (ONGC) won the remaining seven blocks. 
While ONGC was the sole bidder for six blocks, OIL 
was the lone bidder in all the four blocks it bid for. The 
government had offered 11 blocks for exploration and 
production of oil and gas in the fifth bid round under 
the Open Acreage Licensing Policy (OLAP).

Dear Friends,
I hereby greet each and every one on this auspicious occasion 
of 74th Independence Day. We pay respect and tribute to 
all the leaders who has fought for India’s freedom and for 
whom we take pride in being in the world’s largest democratic 
country.
When the struggle for India’s independence was on track, the 
leaders had vowed to promote democracy in free India and to 
remove all the differences based on sex, status and religion. 
India has faced many problems even after independence 
but as a nation we have always tried to solve them and bring 
peace in the country.
The COVID -19 has taken many lives hampered severely the 
economic sector all over the world. Many people have lost 
their jobs even in established and big companies due to 
massive lose in their business. More than lakhs people who 
were working in various parts of the country to bring the bacon 
home returned to their home leaving behind whatever the 
small business or jobs they were involved in. We at Oil India 
Limited as India’s National Company have taken required 
steps to ensure that our operations continue at this critical 
time, while maintaining the COVID-19 protocols. Many of us 
have been directly or indirectly involved in maintaining field 
operations viz. production of oil and gas, drilling activities, 
transportation of oil and gas production of LPG. We Oil-
Indians have shown sheer dedication and commitment for 
the Nation and our courage to face any challenge. I convey 
my sincere gratitude to all.
The Covid pandemic has not yet ended that on 27th May a 
sudden explosion on 5 no. well of OIL, Baghjan happened. As 
a result of the explosion, condensate oil and gas started to 
flow out in a great velocity. The experts of oil were trying to 
bring the situation on control that on 9th June a dangerous 
fire started to blow out in the well. It is to be noted that from 
the starting day of the explosion of gas & condensate oil from 
the well, the experts had continuously pumping water to bring 
down probability of fire breakdown in the well. Nevertheless, 
the fire broke out and killed two of our workers Durlov Gogoi 
& Thikeshwar Gohain, who at that time were working there 
to put down the fire. Sudden demise of the two sincere and 
hardworking fire extinguisher made all of us sad and the CMD 
of OIL Shri Sushil Chandra Mishra declared them martyr and 
provided their families a quick relief.
Now everyday is challenging. But as Oil-Indian, we are stepping 
ahead with patience, full efforts and confidence to overcome 
every challenging time. To fight with the covid crisis, OIL 
has contributed ` 38 Crores to the PM CARES Fund under 
its Corporate Social Responsibility and every Oil Indian has 
also contributed one day salary to the above fund.  OIL is 
undertaking various measures such as: regular sanitization of 
our installations and also the entire Duliajan Township by our 
Fire Service personnel, mass awareness generation campaigns, 
development of sanitizers in-house by our Chemical Deptt 
and distribute and also our medical teams are taking all 
necessary steps.
Our company’s commitment to social welfare is known to 
everyone. Along with exploration and production business our 
company has always been giving importance in development 
of society and the people through various CSR projects. Our 
CSR initiatives touches upon critical thrust areas of society, 
namely Healthcare, Education, Sustainable livelihood, Rural 
infrastructure, Skill development, Women empowerment etc.
On 18th of February, 2020 your company has completed 
61 year . From last few years OIL has been facing various 
challenges I believe together we will overcome all these 
extraordinary challenges. All of you know very well that 
oil and gas industries are the backbone of the economy of 
nation. Country’s economy and the future depends on Oil 
India Company, the second largest oil company of India. 
More than 400 tea garden of Assam and many state level 
company depends on OIL. The existence of AGCL, NEEPCO, 
BVFCL, APL, BCPL etc. are depends on the gas providing by 
OIL. For the sake of development of our region and people 
it is the duty of every Oil Indian, the local people as well as 
various organisations to make Oil India healthy and strong.
Sooner or later this crucial time will be over although the 
impact of Covid will last for a long time. Now we have to 
be patient and contribute towards the development of our 
country as well as our own selves with more determination. 
I hope all of you will continue to extend your support to 
OIL which as an organisation has been playing a key role in 
country’s development.
With this I wish you all again a very Happy Independence Day.
Jai Hind.

OIL commences seismic survey in Andaman Offshore
Oil India Limited, in terms of total proved plus probable 
oil and natural gas reserves, has kick started its offshore 
operations on 8th Oct, 2020 in Andaman Shallow Waters with 
the deployment of a state-of-the-art multi-purpose seismic 
data acquisition vessel “SW Vespucci” for acquisition of 8400 
LK of 2D seismic data in its OALP-III blocks AN-OSHP-2018/1 
& AN-OSHP-2018/2. The exploration efforts in Andaman 
Offshore are being resumed after a long period of time. On 
the Onshore front, OIL was the first company to commence 
2D & 3D seismic data acquisition in any Onshore OALP block 
in the country and also the first company to complete the 
committed seismic work programme in an Onshore OALP 
block. This is in line with the vision of Hon’ble Prime Minister 
of Atmanirbhar Bharat by enhancing domestic exploration 
and production and reduce dependence on imports.
Shri Sushil Chandra Mishra, CMD, OIL, on the occasion 
informed that the company has drawn up plans to intensify 
its exploration efforts in the country and increase exploration 
CAPEX in the coming years. He further stated that the data 
acquisition in Mahanadi on-land blocks in the state of Odisha 
would begin shortly and the exploration drilling campaign in 
its OALP blocks would commence during 2021-22.  OIL plans 
to carry out about 15000 LKM of 2D API, 4000 sq-km of 3D 
API and drill 66 wells in OALP blocks in the next 3-4 years.
OIL was awarded 21 blocks spread over Assam & Assam 
Arakan basin, Rajasthan, Mahanadi Onland, Andaman and 
Kerala-Konkan Offshore basins. Through this process, the 
company has consolidated its position as the leading Operator 
in Northeast with a total acreage area of 17000 sq km. The 
company has been actively participating in the OALP rounds 
in an endeavour to intensify exploration and is expecting two 
more blocks under OALP V in the state of Assam which would 
take the acreage position of OIL in northeast to over 20000 
sq km. Apart from consolidating its position in northeast 
and Rajasthan, OIL has made conscious efforts to carry out 
exploration in Category II & III sedimentary basins in line with 
Government of India’s thrust for exploration.

Blowout at Baghjan Well No. 5
At around 10.30 AM on 27th May, 2020, the producing well 
of Baghjan 5 under Baghjan Oilfield of Oil India Limited in 
Tinsukia district suddenly became very active while work 
over operations were on. As a consequence the ongoing 
operations had to be immediately suspended and the well 
started releasing natural gas in an uncontrolled manner . 
Subsequently, the well caught fire on 9th June, 2020.
Two fire-fighters of OIL died while trying to escape from the 
sudden ignition of the well on 9th June. They drowned in a 
natural water body adjacent to the well, while trying to take 
cover from the inferno.
Around 1610 families were evacuated to safer areas outside 
the 1.5 KM red zone. Relief camps were set up and around 
9000 people stayed in the camps as OIL provided all possible 
care like, food, shelter, mobile dispensary service, etc.  
The fire also burnt around 16 houses and impacted property, 
farmland, vegetation, tea bushes etc. in the nearby area. The 
maximum impact was within 500 metres. Agencies like TERI, 
New Delhi, IIT Guwahati, M/s ERM and NEIST, Jorhat carried 
out various activities at the site, both for measurement of 
environment parameters in impacted areas as well as taking 
up measures like bioremediation for mitigating the impact & 
study of seismic activities at site.
The peripheral fire was contained on 10th June, 2020 and the 
fire and was active only at the well head where gas continued 
to ooze out along with condensate. On 9th Sep, 2020, another 
Officer of OIL died in the line of duty while working at Baghjan 
Well #5. 
Hon’ble Minister of Petroleum and Natural Gas and Steel, 
Shri Dharmendra Pradhan, Hon’ble Chief Minister of 
Assam, Shri Sarbananda Sonowal, Shri Tarun Kapoor, IAS, 
Secretary, MoP&NG 
and several dignitaries 
from Government of 
Assam and Central 
Government had 
visit Baghjan and also 
interacted with the 
local people staying 
in the relief camps. 
Shri Sushil Chandra 
Mishra and all the 
functional directors 
of OIL supervised the 
entire operation to 
control the well and 
related activities.
The Government of Assam carried out various assessment 
studies for measuring the impact on the livelihood and 
property of the impacted population. OIL has also deposited 
emergency relief funds to the affected families as per details 
provided by District Administration. OIL is awaiting the detailed 
report from Government agencies engaged and directive 
from NGT providing final compensation for mitigating the 
impact on the environment as well as loss incurred by the 
affected local population.
Crisis Management Team of OIL, ONGCL and M/s Alert 
from Singapore (6 member team) are carrying out massive 
operations to control the well. Indian Army also provided 
huge support especially in construction of a pontoon bridge 
to help the operations. The well was successfully capped on 
17th August, 2020. With the purpose of reducing the surface 
level well head pressure of the blowout well no 5 at Baghjan, 
the process for restoration of diversion of flow of gas from the 
well head to Baghjan EPS and two flare pits was successful 
implemented on 13th September 2020. 
However, the final killing and control of the well was yet to 
happen. The snubbing unit brought in from Calgary, Canada, to 
kill the gas well in Baghjan reached the site on 4th November 
2020. With all equipment and accessories related to Snubbing 
Operation in place at site, arrangement for placing snubbing 
unit started on 5th November . The team of foreign experts 
and personnel from OIL had begun all the pre-snubbing 
operation activities as planned and the subbing operation 
started on 8th November 2020. The well was finally killed 
with brine solution on 15th November 2020 and the fire 
was doused completely. Further operation to abandon the 
well is in progress.

OIL holds its 61st Annual General Meeting
Oil India Limited (OIL), held its 61st Annual General Body 
(AGM) meeting on 29th Sept 2020 at its Corporate Office in 
Noida. For the very first time in its history of OIL, the AGM 
was successfully conducted on a virtual platform, necessitated 
by the extraordinary demands of the times.
Shri Sushil Chandra Mishra, CMD, OIL along with members 
on the Board including Independent & Government nominee 
directors and shareholders attended the AGM. The CMD 
began his AGM speech to the shareholders by paying heartfelt 
tribute to the three brave hearts of OIL, who lost their lives 
to the unfortunate incident at the Bagjan well#5 Blowout.
As part of the AGM, an online Press meet was also conducted, 
where CMD and the members on the board interacted with 
journalists from reputed media houses both from regional 
as well as National media.
Fiscal 2019-20- a year of achievements as well as significant 
challenges for the Company
• Reported Revenue of ` 13,649 crores and profit after 
tax of ` 2,584 crores.
• Annual domestic production of crude oil was 3.133 MMT 
and domestic natural gas production was 2801 MMSCM.
• Due to the success of Cyclic Steam Stimulation process 
implemented in 2018-19, production from Rajasthan 
Field for the year 2019-20 increased by 168%.
• Interim dividend of Rs. 9 per share was declared and 
paid in the month of February 2020.
• Received the highest ever dividend from overseas 
business.
• Became the first Operator to commence exploration 
activities in any OALP Block in the country by commencing 
2D & 3D survey in Rajasthan OALP Block.
• First Operator to complete seismic acquisition in any 
OALP Block in the country as per the committed work 
programme by completing the seismic acquisition in 
North Bank OALP Block in the District of Dhemaji, Assam.
• Completed first well (well KMC-15) in Kumchai Field 
in Arunachal Pradesh after the resumption the drilling 
activities, which was kept in abeyance since 2003 due 
to non-availability of PML.
• Received “NIL” comments for its accounts from the 
Comptroller and Auditor General of India for the 
eighteenth consecutive year .
• Developed in-house EOR screening software, based on 
SPE 12069 and Taber et al SPE 35385, which resulted in 
EOR screening of 209 reservoirs of Oil India Limited.
• Developed new R&D Formulation for wellbore cleaning 
and oil well stimulation using combination of solvents that 
are effective for both asphaltic and paraffinic deposits.
• Maintained international credit ratings in-spite of high 
volatility of oil prices. The company has also retained 
the highest Domestic credit rating for long term as well 
as for short term credit.

In his address to the shareholders, the CMD described financial 
year 2019-20 as a challenging one with weak global economic 
growth, resulting in weaker demands across sectors. Lower 
average crude oil prices, as compared to the previous year,

in the main producing area, especially in the last quarter of 
2019-20. However, he mentioned that despite the difficulties, 
OIL completed the year 2019-20 on a positive note, leveraging 
the best of available advantages and building on available 
opportunities.

Home Based Digital Learning Model 
for rural students launched under Project Dikhya
In view of the recent COVID-19 pandemic and subsequent 
closure of educational institutions, for the 1st time an 
innovative intervention called ‘Home Based Digital Learning 
Model’ has been successfully launched on 17th September 
2020 for the students of rural areas of classes VI, VII and VIII of 
existing 30 schools under CSR Project ‘OIL Dikhya’ (Earlier in a 
pre-COVID situation, education under OIL Dikhya was imparted 
in the 30 schools via mobile computer labs cum libraries, 
smart classrooms and other pedagogic methodologies). 
The said alternative intervention in the absence of physical 
learning in schools will focus on holistic development by 
addressing the gaps in academic learning (for subjects like 
Science, Maths, English and Computer Sciences), Life Skills 
Education, Financial Literacy, Socio-Emotional Learning and 
various other pertinent topics for students as well as parents. 
Therefore, keeping in view the lockdown rules laid down by 
government in schools for containing the virus and to provide 
continuous learning impetus with a newer dimension for 
bringing the existing gaps, the ‘Home Based Digital Learning 
Model’ is inbuilt with customized and accessible technology 
based educational solutions covering around 7,000 students 
along with their parents. The intervention is pertinent in 
view of keeping alive the zest of knowledge amongst the 
students which otherwise has been hindered due to the 
pandemic situation.
In the context of students of OIL’s operational areas and 
focussing on inclusion & equity, the activity-based model 
is driven by a blended approach for delivering customized 
content through a three-tier methodology of: (a) Digital (b) 
Telephonic & (c) Community Mentor Support. Further, the 
digital & telephonic lessons are driven by two approaches: 
(i) IVR (Interactive Voice Response) based Community 
Media Platform through non-data mobile penetration and 
(ii) Internet-based lesson videos shared through Whatsapp/
YouTube, etc. The videos of the uploaded lessons are being 
made by the teachers from a classroom setting using K-Yan 
& K-Class.
