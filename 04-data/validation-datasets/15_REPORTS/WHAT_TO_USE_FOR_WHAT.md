# What to use for what

- SIF precursor understanding: IOGP narratives, NIOSH FACE/FOG, BSEE investigations.
- Fatal/severe examples: NIOSH FACE, NIOSH FOG, IOGP fatal narratives, OSHA SIR.
- Life-Saving Rule classification: official IOGP Life-Saving Rules reference and IOGP narratives.
- Causal/barrier extraction: BSEE investigations, NIOSH FACE, IOGP narratives, Oil India near-miss procedure schema.
- Oil India context: OIL BRSR, official HSE pages, public near-miss procedure, Baghjan sources.
- Semantic similarity/clustering: narrative-bearing IOGP/NIOSH/BSEE/OSHA records after leakage review.
- External validation: locked IOGP 2025.
- Dashboard statistics: OIL BRSR aggregates and BSEE statistics, clearly marked aggregate/statistics-only.

Never automatically make negatives from high-potential, fatality, severe injury, near miss, no injury, or minor consequence. Keep IOGP guides, Life-Saving Rules, FOG documentation, and schema material reference-only.
