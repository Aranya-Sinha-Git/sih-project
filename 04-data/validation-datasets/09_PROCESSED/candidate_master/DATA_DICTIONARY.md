# Canonical data dictionary

Fields are source-preserving where available. `sif_potential` and `sif_label_status` are intentionally unresolved for records without an expert/source-native SIF adjudication. `high_potential` is never converted to `sif_potential`. Values absent from a source remain blank.

The master currently includes the filtered OSHA SIR candidate view. BSEE and FACE remain in source-specific/reference views until their native schemas and incident grain are reconciled.
